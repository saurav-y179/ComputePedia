x86 / x86-64

x86 is a processor architecture widely used in personal computers, laptops, workstations, and servers. x86-64 is the 64-bit extension used by modern processors from Intel and AMD. Its long history gives it broad software compatibility, while current chips combine high clock speeds, multiple cores, and specialized features for gaming, creative work, and data centers.
