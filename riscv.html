RISC-V

RISC-V is an open instruction set architecture that can be freely implemented and customized. It is used in embedded systems, research, education, and increasingly in commercial processors. Its modular design lets chip makers choose the features they need, making it useful for everything from small controllers to experimental laptops and high-performance computing projects.
